# 🌱 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The project welcomes constructive participation in issues, pull requests, reviews, and documentation discussions. Everyone is expected to help maintain a respectful, inclusive, and useful environment.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and in good faith.
- Critique the work or proposal, not the person.
- Keep feedback factual, specific, and actionable.
- Respect different technical perspectives.
- Protect personal and confidential information.
- Give appropriate credit where ownership or attribution is verified.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or repeated bad-faith disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, confidential datasets, or private exports.
- Deliberately sabotaging project discussions or contributions.
- Retaliation against someone reporting a concern in good faith.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community. Actions should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to Brajesh Kumar (`@Brajesh31`) through a private GitHub contact channel. Provide relevant context where safe and avoid sharing unrelated personal information.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents or participates in this project.
